EVLoc AI / About

Built to answer one question.

Where should the next EV charging station be opened? EVLoc AI turns multiple location signals into a simple, explainable opportunity ranking.

01

Demand signal

Historical EV charging demand is used to train a Random Forest regression model. The model can estimate demand for different times and conditions.

02

Location signal

EV population, traffic, current stations, station gap, distance and accessibility describe the market opportunity around each area.

03

Business score

The final 0–100 score makes it easier to compare areas and identify where a new station deserves attention first.
